Design principles

Small libraries. Clear boundaries.

01

Composable by design

GameKits can be used together, but none of them needs the entire family to be useful.

02

Game-agnostic core

Reusable mechanics and infrastructure stay separate from product-specific rules and UX.

03

Practical APIs

The public surface should stay understandable for a game developer without exposing every internal detail.
